About us

At Plei, we envision a world where the beautiful game of soccer unites communities and fosters connections across the globe. We're committed to leveraging technology to make soccer accessible to everyone, so community and camaraderie are within reach for all.

Our passion drives us to create a platform synonymous with play, where every match brings people closer together. We believe that by providing better access to soccer, we can cultivate a stronger, more connected world. And we're dedicated to upholding the values of our community, celebrating every story of joy and connection made through Plei.

As we grow, we aspire to be more than a pickup soccer app, and become a catalyst for positive global impact through the beautiful game.

Plei's origin story

Plei's inception traces back to the early years of Sebastian Duque, our CEO, whose passion for soccer has been a defining element of his life. From a young age, soccer was more than a pastime for Sebastian; it was a profound part of his identity, whether he was playing, spectating, or engaging in the digital realm through FIFA.

After graduating from college, Sebastian returned to Miami and resumed playing soccer with his friends. He soon identified a common issue: while many people shared his enthusiasm for the game, organizing matches and finding fellow players was a complex task. Beyond the logistical hurdles, there was a notable lack of accessible platforms for people without existing teams or networks to connect and play.

Recognizing this gap, Sebastian conceived the idea of a centralized platform to facilitate soccer engagement, a hub where enthusiasts could effortlessly coordinate games based on their preferred schedules and locations. When he presented the concept to his brother Alejandro, it resonated deeply, and Alejandro decided to join forces with him.

Thus Plei was founded by Sebastian and Alejandro Duque, driven by a shared vision to revolutionize the soccer experience, blending their passion for the sport with the strategic insight gained from their academic and professional experiences. The journey of Plei is a testament to their perseverance, innovative spirit, and commitment to a community-centered platform that makes soccer more accessible.

Through Plei, the Duque brothers aspire to foster connections, promote inclusivity, and elevate the joy of soccer, a broader mission to make the game a unifying force that brings people together from all walks of life, united by their love for the beautiful game.
