Purpose, mission & values

Who we are and what we stand for.

⚽ Purpose

To create a platform where soccer enthusiasts can easily find and participate in games, building a stronger, more connected community through a shared passion for the sport.

🚀 Mission

Connect the world through the beautiful game of soccer, using technology to make community and play accessible to everyone, everywhere. Truly becoming the largest soccer platform and community in the world.

🔭 Vision

Plei is the global marketplace uniting 5 billion soccer enthusiasts, connecting players, game organizers, and brand partners worldwide.

Core values

  • People first. Always. We lead with empathy, inclusion, and joy, on and off the pitch. We build for the humans who power our ecosystem: players, organizers, partners, and teammates.
  • We're building the world's soccer marketplace. This isn't an app, it's a movement. Every decision should unlock value across the player, organizer, and partner triangle.
  • Craft with purpose. Operate with discipline. Our work must be clean, sharp, and useful, because that's how we scale. Every decision connects back to a real user need or strategic goal.
  • Innovation at our core. We embrace AI and emerging tools as fundamental forces. We think in systems, not features, always asking what unlocks the most value next.
  • Bias toward action, built to learn. We ship to learn. We move fast, stay curious, and aren't afraid of being wrong. Mistakes are feedback.
  • Respect and transparency are non-negotiable. We communicate clearly and honestly, even when it's hard. We listen, ask why, and assume good intent.
  • Team of owners. We hold each other accountable, strive for excellence, and support one another without codependence. A place for growth, ownership, and performance.
  • Global talent, local impact. We hire across borders, cultures, and experiences. What unites us is ambition, resilience, and a shared vision to make soccer accessible, social, and smarter.

These values guide our interactions, decisions, and the way we operate as a team.
