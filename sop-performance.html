Performance Reviews & Feedback

Applies to: Contributors, managers, and select contractors Owner: HR Questions: HR@plei.com

Your go-to spot for mid-year and end-of-year reviews. The process is two-way, goal-oriented, and actionable, so every person knows where they stand and how to grow.

Templates

  • Team Member Self-Review Template, complete before your 1:1.
  • Manager Review Template, guides your evaluation and feedback.
  • Anonymous Feedback for Managers.
  • Anonymous Feedback for Peers, optional.
  • U.S. Independent Contractor Check-In Template.

Schedule

  • Mid-year reviews: January–June reviews due by June 27.
  • End-of-year reviews: July–December reviews due by December 8.

Regular 1:1s still happen throughout the year for ongoing feedback. Managers should set and track goals at the start of each cycle so evaluations tie to clear expectations.

For team members

  1. Duplicate the Self-Review Template into your private workspace.
  2. Complete it: key accomplishments, areas for growth, progress toward goals, goals for the next 6 months, support needed, and honest feedback for your manager.
  3. Submit to your manager 3 days before your scheduled review.
  4. Be prepared to discuss your self-assessment in the meeting.

For managers

  1. Review the team member's self-assessment first, starting with their "Feedback for Your Manager" section.
  2. Duplicate the Manager Review Template and complete: wins and impact, areas for improvement, progress on past goals, new goals, and development/mentorship plans.
  3. Incorporate peer or contractor feedback as applicable.
  4. Before the due date, upload all completed reviews to your department folder in Drive: Performance Reviews → Completed Reviews → (Department) → 2026.
  5. Host a live 1:1 to review both documents together.
  6. Discuss openly, confirm action items, and share the final document with the employee.

Tips & best practices

  • Two-way: both employees and managers give feedback to each other.
  • Keep it focused: thoughtful, concise responses; don't make it burdensome.
  • Peer reviews: optional but encouraged.
  • Goal alignment: tie reviews to OKRs/KPIs set at the start of the cycle.
  • Maintain transparency; focus on results, impact, and ownership.
The live review platform is at performance.plei.com. Questions: HR@plei.com.
